title: 'Expanding AI Literacy: How Emotional Intelligence Supports Productive Uncertainty'
abstract: This paper presents a conceptual framework for understanding the role of
  uncertainty in the context of LLM usage in education. We argue that AI does not
  eliminate uncertainty but displaces it, often reducing epistemic uncertainty while
  amplifying its metacognitive and emotional forms. Throughout the paper, we use hypothetical
  examples to illustrate the theoretical concepts discussed, although these examples
  are not meant to provide a predictive account of student outcomes. Finally, we present
  emotional intelligence skills as a vital but often-overlooked component of AI literacy,
  helping students recognize the uncertainty that arises in co-creation with LLMs
  not as a cause for unproductive anxiety, but as a catalyst for creativity.
